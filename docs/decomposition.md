# Today in AI Process Decomposition

1. Read the standing editorial, image, and publishing instructions.
2. Build the rolling review for the previous seven calendar days.
3. Read the recent story headings, hooks, and meme families, then visually inspect each listed final image.
4. Check the previous successful run and set the current-news research window.
5. Confirm Postiz authentication and account routing for LinkedIn and X, then check the current-date receipts and platform state for duplicates.
6. Scan the main official and reporting accounts on X first and save direct status URLs. Open the primary source behind each candidate, then search every current AI newsletter in `hi@paytonbilodeau.com`, including a mailbox-wide sweep for new recurring senders beyond the standing list. Read relevant editions in full and capture their story choices, source links, explanations, and limitations. Use those notes with official AI newsrooms and broad independent AI desks to rank the field and catch misses. Review at least twelve current leads or document that fewer existed after every required surface was checked.
7. Verify material claims and cut unsupported items.
8. Build the weighted candidate slate in `sources.md`. Treat freshness as eligibility, not importance. Lead with the highest consequential verified story unless a specific evidence-based disqualification is recorded, then run the obvious-biggest-story challenge before writing.
9. Write a plain-language factual draft with event, contradiction, mechanism, limitation, and consequence.
10. Push the humor closer to Fireship with one strong situational premise per suitable story and two to four funny lines in a normal edition. Allow a sharper punchline, absurd comparison, escalating list, understatement, or one callback when the verified mechanism earns it. Do not copy a Fireship or newsletter phrase, joke, analogy, or persona, and use restraint around real harm.
11. Put a full blank line after the title, every heading, and every sentence or short thought. Read the copy aloud and fix broken connective reasoning, repeated formulae, stacked facts, attached-on jokes, announced importance, and cliché endings. Keep every research URL and citation in `sources.md`; public `copy.txt` must contain no URL, `Sources:` footer, citation list, or source link.
12. Refresh the Fireship corpus when stale, read its thumbnail synthesis, open both contact sheets, complete the daily image brief, and source the recognizable meme, directly relevant public figure, press, licensed, public-domain, or owned asset.
13. Choose reference-aware generation or a professionally integrated background-removed cutout.
14. Build schema-version-2 `image-assets.json` with source hashes, usage notes, production route, exact AI Mentorship palette, heavy type, locked publication-mark geometry, and official logo sources.
15. Build and QA the 1920 by 1080 image from supplied subject and official-logo references. Require every in-scene logo to be fully contained by its intended object's usable surface and to follow that surface's plane. A logo on a keyboard key must stay inside the keycap's top face without crossing its bevel or gaps. Add exact headline type after the base visual is approved.
16. Apply the transparent Today in AI master with `execution/today_in_ai_badge.py` at exactly 230 pixels wide and 84 pixels from the left and bottom.
17. Run the asset validator and deterministic rolling seven-day novelty check. Revise until both pass.
18. Run `today_in_ai_prepare.py --dry-run`.
19. Save the complete dated package, including `image-assets.json` and `recent-seven-review.md`.
20. Build, validate, and render separate LinkedIn and X manifests.
21. Publish LinkedIn and X separately through Postiz.
22. Verify both Postiz results, live URLs, release IDs, receipts, and attached media.
23. Save both Postiz receipts and both verified states in `publish-results.json` and the edition log.
24. After both destinations are verified, run the retention check. Every 14 days it moves Desktop delivery copies older than the rolling seven-day window to Trash.
25. Run deterministic wrapper validation before reporting success.
