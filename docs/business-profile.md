# Today in AI Business Profile

## Publisher

Payton Bilodeau publishes Today in AI from his personal LinkedIn and X accounts.

## Audience

Intelligent non-technical adults, mainly ages 30 to 60+, who are curious about AI and want a short, accurate explanation without hype or homework.

## Product

A daily briefing with one or two verified AI stories by default, three when the day earns them, concise event-to-mechanism explanations, Fireship-paced situational humor that can occasionally be bold, and one original 16:9 image. Four stories are exceptional.

## Brand Position

Rationally optimistic about AI, honest about limits, politically neutral, practical, and easy to understand.

## Distribution

- LinkedIn alias: `linkedin`
- X alias: `x`
- Publishing control layer: `execution/social_publisher.py`
- LinkedIn and X backend: Postiz

## Business Goal

Build trust and a consistent personal audience by publishing a useful edition every morning.
