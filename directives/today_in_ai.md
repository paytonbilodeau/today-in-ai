# Today in AI editorial and delivery rules

Version 1.1.0 uses the [production packet](../prompts/today-in-ai-automation-prompt.md).
Complete [your run configuration](../prompts/RUN-TEMPLATE.md) privately. Draft-only
is the starting authority; a scheduled trigger alone does not authorize publication.

Choose current, consequential stories using recorded primary evidence and the
weighted slate. Explain what changed, how it works, the limitation and the useful
consequence. Preserve factual meaning while writing original, accessible humor.
Keep research links outside the public copy and do not invent a hands-on test.

New images use schema 3. Generate text and style-matched marks with the complete
scene, using current official structural references. Inspect recognizable logo
identity and surface integration at full and phone size. Native edits repair
generated defects. Preserve authentic screenshots and original brand assets.
Schema 1/2 support and the badge tool remain for historical inspection only.

Current packaging labels the publication and requires a single X post. Validate
and render locally, check live account routing, submit X first, and wait for its
terminal PUBLISHED state before LinkedIn. Preserve pending and accepted journals;
an uncertain action cannot be treated as failed and submitted again. Provider
acceptance is separate from terminal status and independent public verification.

No YouTube publication or missed-date replay belongs in this workflow. Keep the
newsletter/social destinations and retention permissions explicit. Preserve the
complete local package when a gate or tool fails and report the exact repair step.
